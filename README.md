# 🌞 Perfect Day

**Build your own perfect day.** Pick an hour, a weather and a season, and the
whole valley repaints itself — sky, sun, hills, wildlife and sound.

Then stay a while. There are facts to flip, riddles to lose sleep over, a game
to be bad at, and a Wordsworth sonnet at the bottom.

> Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No React, no npm, no build step.
> Double-click the file and it runs.

---

## 🎛️ Three dials, sixty-four days

Everything lives in one small panel in the corner. Three dials, and the
important part is that **none of them knows about the others**:

| | pick from | in charge of |
|---|---|---|
| 🌅 **the hour** | sunrise · daytime · sunset · night | the sky and the sun |
| 🌦️ **the weather** | clear · rain · breeze · seaside | the air |
| 🍂 **the season** | spring · summer · autumn · winter | the ground |

4 × 4 × 4 = **64 different days**, and every one is a real combination rather
than a preset someone drew. Which means it can rain at midnight. It should,
and it does.

Drag the sun slider and the sun really travels — the hour follows it, so the
sky can never disagree with where the sun is. Or press **follow the real
time** and let it sit wherever it actually is right now, moving on its own.

Owls only come out at night. Snowmen only turn up in winter. The butterflies
have the good sense to go indoors when it rains.

## 🔊 There are no sound files in here

Four channels — 🌧️ rain, 🍃 breeze, 🌊 waves, 🐦 birds — all built live in your
browser out of filtered noise and very slow wobbles. Nothing is downloaded,
nothing loops, and you never hear the same second twice.

The mix retunes itself every time you change the day, and then the moment you
touch a slider it becomes *yours* and stops interfering.

Five ready-made ones if you'd rather not fiddle:
⛈️ storm · 🌻 garden · 🏖️ shore · ⛰️ hill · 🤫 shhh

## ⭐ Things to find

- **Six flip cards** on the small physics of a nice day — why sunrise is red,
  and why every drawing of a raindrop you have ever seen is a lie
- **Six riddles**, with a hint if you get properly stuck
- **Sunflower Catcher** — catch 🌻🍀🦋🍎🌈🌟, dodge 🐝 and 🌩️, grab 🧲 🛡️ ⏳ 💎 💗.
  Three hearts and then it's over.
- **A star jar** in the corner that fills up as you poke about
- **An about page**, which is the actual answer to the question the rest of
  the site keeps asking
- **A sixty-fifth day**, hidden under the sonnet: Westminster Bridge, the 3rd
  of September 1802, the one morning with a city in it
