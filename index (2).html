I turn up without making a sound, and I'm still the loudest thing that happens all morning. Everything you can see was already there — I just hand it back. What am I?

02 easy

I'm shortest when the day is at its very best, longest when it's nearly over, and I vanish the second the light does. I copy everything you do and never once say why. What am I?

step four · the real answer

💛 A perfect day, in her own words

A perfect day for me starts with waking up early to the sound of birds and a soft breeze coming through the window. I enjoy a warm cup of tea while watching the sunrise, feeling calm and refreshed. After that, I go for a peaceful morning walk, taking in nature and clearing my mind.

The rest of the day is spent doing things I love — reading a good book, listening to music, and maybe spending time with close friends or family. A small outing, like going to a quiet café or a nearby park, makes it even better. There’s no rush, no stress — just simple moments that bring joy.

In the evening, I watch a favorite movie or sit under the stars, reflecting on the day. A perfect day doesn’t need to be grand — it’s about peace, connection, and doing what makes the heart feel full.

step five · somebody beat us to it

📜 The first perfect day on record

On the 3rd of September 1802, William Wordsworth walked over a bridge in London early enough that the city hadn't started yet — and stopped dead. That sun, and those rays up there, come straight out of these 14 lines. Press the button underneath and the valley becomes his view: the bridge, the river, and the city with nothing switched on yet. ☀️

The whole poem turns on one line: never did sun more beautifully steep in his first splendour. He's not describing scenery — he's describing a place with nothing switched on yet, and finding it better than anything he'd seen in the mountains. 🏔️

Which is sort of the argument here too. A perfect day doesn't need anything in it. It mainly needs you awake, outside, and looking the right way. 🌻
